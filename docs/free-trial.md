# Free trial report

The free trial on [salisec.ir](https://salisec.ir) lets you see what SaliSecure finds on your own site before buying the full report.

## What the trial shows

- **Real verdict and charts** for your site: score, grade, severity distribution, radar chart.
- **The total number of findings**, with real severity counts (how many critical, high, medium, low).
- **One or two fully explained weaknesses** — the highest-risk findings that were directly observed and that you can re-check yourself — with their impact and fix.
- Real counts for coverage (how many checks ran) and a few headline values such as certificate status and WAF presence.

## What stays locked

For every other finding the preview shows only the severity and the count. The title, impact, evidence, remediation and reproduction commands **are not present in the file**, not merely hidden or blurred; they cannot be recovered from the PDF. Detailed matrices (headers, ports, cookies, TLS, DNS) are masked, and addresses and banners are partly masked.

## What the full report adds

- All findings with technical detail, evidence and impact
- Step-by-step remediation for each issue, ranked P0 to P4
- Unmasked header, TLS / certificate, DNS, port and cookie matrices
- Endpoint, API and form inventory and the findings appendix
- Reproduction commands so your team can verify every fix
- The executive one-page summary (Deep scan)

## Using the trial

1. Make sure you own the domain or have written permission to test it ([safety and authorization](safety-and-authorization.md)).
2. Request the trial at [salisec.ir](https://salisec.ir).
3. Quote the **scan id** printed on the report if you order the full version; it identifies exactly the assessment you saw.
